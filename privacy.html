Privacy Policy for Pivora

Last updated: September 13, 2026
Pivora is built with an offline-first philosophy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, documents, or usage analytics.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Pivora iOS mobile application developed by Ata Eryılmaz. Your privacy is a fundamental right, and Pivora is designed to operate completely on your device without communicating with any external servers.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

We collect zero data. Pivora does not contain any third-party tracking frameworks, analytics tools, or advertising SDKs. Everything you do inside the app stays strictly on your physical device.

3. Document Processing & Storage

  • Local Sandbox: All PDF documents, scans, signatures, and draft projects are stored in your device's secure local sandbox.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Files are never uploaded to any cloud servers or third-party storage.
  • Document Sharing: When you share an exported PDF via the iOS Share Sheet or AirDrop, the transfer is handled entirely by Apple's built-in operating system mechanisms.

4. Device Permissions

Pivora may request access to certain device features solely to enable specific offline tools:

  • Camera: Used strictly to take photos of paper documents for scanning into PDF. Camera data is processed in real time on-device and never saved externally.
  • Photo Library: Used only when you choose to import an image into a document or save exported PDF pages as JPEG images.
  • Speech & Audio: The audio reader feature uses Apple's native on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer to read document text out loud. No audio or text is sent over the internet.

5. Children's Privacy

Pivora does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for all audiences.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
